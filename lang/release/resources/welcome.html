Mycel 0.1 alpha

A friendly language for your first program and your hundredth.

This preview installs mycel and ply in /usr/local/bin. Node is included. Requires macOS 13.5 or later on Apple silicon.

Create hello.myc containing print("hi"), then run: mycel hello.myc
